FAQ

Good to know

Do I have to attend live?

These are a better value live — we work through your personal AI builds together and create a culture of feedback and community with other business owners.

What if I can only make one or two sessions?

Single sessions are $100 each. We don't sell recordings of this series — the value is in building live, in real time.

What tools do I need?

You need access to whatever tool is being used in that session (ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini — Google Workspace for full capability). No access? You're still welcome to attend, watch, and ask questions — you just won't leave with a build of your own.

How do I join the sessions?

These run live on Zoom. After you register, you'll get a confirmation email with your personal Zoom registration link and a calendar invite for each session you're signed up for.

How do I claim my Founding Cohort coaching call?

You'll get a scheduling link within 24 hours of registering.
